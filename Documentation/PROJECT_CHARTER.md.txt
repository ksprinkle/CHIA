# Community Health Intelligence Atlas (CHIA)

# Project Charter

## Mission

Develop an offline, research-grade atlas that integrates authoritative public health, demographic, socioeconomic, and healthcare data to better understand patterns of community health burden across the United States.

---

# Objectives

CHIA will:

* Measure overall community health burden.
* Identify geographic patterns and clusters.
* Compare similar communities.
* Identify resilient communities that outperform expectations.
* Provide transparent, reproducible analysis.
* Support research and informed decision-making.

---

# Out of Scope (Version 1)

CHIA will not:

* Diagnose individuals.
* Predict individual health outcomes.
* Provide medical advice.
* Infer causation from correlation.
* Require cloud hosting or user accounts.
* Become a continuously maintained web platform.

---

# Geographic Scope

* United States only
* Primary unit: Counties
* Future versions may include Census Tracts and ZCTAs.

---

# Guiding Values

* Transparency
* Scientific integrity
* Reproducibility
* Simplicity
* Practical usefulness

---

# Version 1 Deliverables

* Health Burden Index
* County rankings
* Community profiles
* Similar county analysis
* Excel workbook
* Offline HTML atlas
* Executive report
* Methodology
* Data dictionary

---

# Definition of Done

Version 1 is complete when all planned deliverables are documented, reproducible, functional offline, and capable of answering the project's primary research questions.

---

# Future Vision

Future enhancements will only be considered after Version 1 is complete and will be evaluated against the Project Principles before being added.
